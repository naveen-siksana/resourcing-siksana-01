# Resourcing Module

This module handles all resource management operations for the system.

## Submodules

### Employees
- Employee profile management
- Employee onboarding/offboarding
- Contact information
- Employment history

### Projects
- Project creation and management
- Project timelines and milestones
- Project budgets and costs
- Project status tracking

### Allocations
- Resource allocation to projects
- Capacity planning
- Allocation forecasting
- Utilization tracking

### Timesheets
- Timesheet entry and submission
- Timesheet approval workflows
- Time tracking by project/task
- Billable vs. non-billable hours

### Skills
- Skills inventory management
- Skill assessments
- Training and development tracking
- Skills gap analysis

### Departments
- Department structure and hierarchy
- Department budget management
- Department resource allocation
- Department reporting
