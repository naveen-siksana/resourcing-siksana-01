# Financials Module

This module handles all financial management operations for the resourcing system.

## Submodules

### Budgets
- Budget creation and management
- Budget allocation across projects and departments
- Budget tracking and monitoring
- Budget vs. actual analysis

### Invoices
- Invoice generation and management
- Invoice approval workflows
- Payment tracking
- Invoice templates

### Expenses
- Expense submission and approval
- Expense categories and policies
- Receipt management
- Expense reimbursement tracking

### Payroll
- Payroll processing
- Salary calculations
- Tax and deductions management
- Payroll reports

### Reports
- Financial reporting
- Custom report generation
- Scheduled reports
- Export functionality

### Forecasting
- Financial projections
- Revenue forecasting
- Cost forecasting
- Scenario analysis
